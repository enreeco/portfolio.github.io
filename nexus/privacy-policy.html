Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This policy explains how the NEXUS desktop application for Windows, macOS and Linux ("NEXUS", "the app"), including its nexus command-line tool, handles your information. In short: your data stays on your computer, and we never receive it. It leaves your computer only if you choose a backup destination, such as a cloud folder or your own Google Drive.

1. What NEXUS stores

NEXUS stores what you enter — tasks, projects and sections, comments, relations, contacts, custom fields, attached files, settings — in SQLite database files on your computer, one per profile, inside the app's data folder or a location you choose. A small config.json in the same data folder lists your profiles and app settings, such as the name your comments are signed with. If you connect Google Drive (section 4), the data folder also holds a gdrive-tokens.json file with the access it was given (on macOS and Linux, readable only by your user account).

2. No account, no tracking

NEXUS has no account of its own and needs no sign-in to work. It does not send your data, usage statistics, crash reports or any other information to us or to third parties, and it works fully offline. The app connects to the internet only when you ask it to: to back up to Google Drive (section 4), or to open a link in your browser (section 6).

3. Backups to a folder you choose

If you set a backup folder, NEXUS writes copies (snapshots) of your database into it. When that folder is synchronised by a service such as Dropbox, OneDrive or iCloud Drive, the copies are uploaded by that service, under its own terms and privacy policy. NEXUS does not talk to those services itself, and you can turn these backups off at any time.

4. Backups to Google Drive (optional)

You can connect a profile to your own Google Drive, in Settings → Profiles → Backups → Google Drive or with nexus snapshot drive connect. Nothing happens with Google until you do.

  • Sign-in. Connecting opens Google's sign-in page in your web browser. You sign in with Google, not with us; NEXUS never sees your Google password.
  • What NEXUS may access. NEXUS asks only for the drive.file permission: it can see and change only the files and folders it creates itself (a Nexus backups folder with one sub-folder per profile), nothing else in your Drive. It also reads your Google account's email address and name, to show which account a profile is connected to; only the email address is kept, on your computer.
  • What goes to your Drive. Snapshots of the profile's database — which contain everything you stored in that profile — are uploaded from your computer straight to your own, personal Google Drive, where they are kept under Google's terms and privacy policy. They stay in your Google account: nobody who makes NEXUS can see, open or use them. NEXUS lists them there to offer restores and downloads, and to tell you when another of your computers has published a newer one.
  • The token service. To complete the sign-in, and later to renew the app's access, NEXUS sends the one-time code Google returns (and later the renewal token) to a small service we run at nexus-proxy.organizer.solutions, on Cloudflare. It adds the app's Google credentials and passes the request on to Google, so that those credentials do not have to ship inside the app. It only relays: it saves nothing — no codes, no tokens, no logs of them — and it never receives your database, your backups or the contents of your Drive, so it cannot give anyone access to them. Cloudflare processes these requests, including your IP address, under its own privacy policy.
  • Disconnecting. Disconnect makes NEXUS forget the access and the folder on this computer; the backups stay in your Drive until you delete them. You can withdraw NEXUS's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

5. Google user data

NEXUS uses the information it receives from Google — your account's email address and name, and the backup files it created in your Drive — only to provide the backup features described above, on your computer. We do not receive it, sell it, share it with anyone, use it for advertising, or use it to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models. NEXUS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Links you open

Links you add to tasks open in your web browser when you click them; what happens there is governed by the site you visit.

7. AI agents and the command line

The nexus command and the agent skills that come with NEXUS work on your computer, on the same databases as the app. If you let an AI agent use them, what the agent reads from NEXUS is handled by that agent and its provider, under their terms and privacy policy — NEXUS does not send it anywhere itself.

8. Downloads and this website

The installers are hosted on GitHub. Downloading them from this site is subject to GitHub's privacy statement. This website does not use cookies or analytics.

9. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays until you delete it: remove tasks or profiles in the app, use Settings → Data → Database → Reset Database, or delete the database files and the app's data folder. Uninstalling the app does not delete your databases, so you do not lose them by accident. Backups in your folder or in the Nexus backups folder of your Google Drive are yours to keep or delete.

10. Security

Your data is as safe as your computer and your accounts: keep your system up to date, protect your user account and your Google account, and be careful where you share exports and backups, which contain your data unencrypted. The Google Drive access file lives in your user account's data folder: anyone who can use that account can use it too.

11. Children

NEXUS is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
