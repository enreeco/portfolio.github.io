Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This policy explains how the NEXUS desktop application for Windows, macOS and Linux ("NEXUS", "the app"), including its nexus command-line tool, handles your information. In short: your data stays on your computer, and we never receive it.

1. What NEXUS stores

NEXUS stores what you enter — tasks, projects and sections, comments, relations, contacts, custom fields, attached files, settings — in SQLite database files on your computer, one per profile, inside the app's data folder or a location you choose. A small config.json in the same data folder lists your profiles and app settings, such as the name your comments are signed with.

2. No servers, no account, no tracking

NEXUS has no account and no sign-in. It does not send your data, usage statistics, crash reports or any other information to us or to third parties, and it does not contact the internet to work. We do not operate servers that receive data from the app.

3. Backups you choose

If you set a snapshot folder, NEXUS writes copies of your database into it. When that folder is synchronised by a service such as Dropbox, OneDrive or iCloud Drive, the copies are uploaded by that service, under its own terms and privacy policy. NEXUS does not talk to those services itself, and you can turn snapshots off at any time.

4. Links you open

Links you add to tasks open in your web browser when you click them; what happens there is governed by the site you visit.

5. Downloads

The installers are hosted on GitHub. Downloading them from this site is subject to GitHub's privacy statement. This website does not use cookies or analytics.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays until you delete it: remove tasks or profiles in the app, use Settings → Database Tools → Reset Database, or delete the database files and the app's data folder. Uninstalling the app does not delete your databases, so you do not lose them by accident. Snapshot copies in your cloud folder are yours to keep or delete.

7. Security

Your data is as safe as your computer and your accounts: keep your system up to date, protect your user account, and be careful where you share exports, which contain your data in plain text.

8. Children

NEXUS is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
